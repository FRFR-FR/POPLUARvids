- Still photos are given slow pan/zoom movement. They are clearly still-image sequences, not presented as moving footage.
- No sources, licence notices or technical explanations are shown inside the video.
- Every media item is attributed here; the generated build appends the exact Pixabay contributor URLs from its artifact metadata.
